## Not replaced (the real app uses icons here)

- Taxonomy list and grid thumbnails (Collections, Order list/grid/FAB menu): the app shows a silhouette icon or the ANTZ "a" mark on a grey tile, so these stay as icons.
- Animal record thumbnails on Administer: the app shows the ANTZ "a" mark (no photo uploaded), so these stay as the mark.
- Avatars ("DA", "NZ", "SO"): the app uses initials on a coloured circle, not photos.
